Pliego app icon (designcraft.svg, designcraft-small.svg and every file derived from them in this
directory, and the web favicon where the project has one).

The icon is a portrait of Johannes Gutenberg cut out, converted to black and white and placed over the
app colour. It belongs to the Nodalia icon family: one historical figure per app.

Source portrait: "Portret van Johannes Gutenberg, RP-P-1914-4009.jpg", from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Portret_van_Johannes_Gutenberg,_RP-P-1914-4009.jpg).
Status of the source: CC0.
